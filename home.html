Features

Built around the chemotherapy cycle

◎

Chemo plans per patient

Set the regimen, number of cycles and interval with multiple drugs and default doses, then adjust any individual cycle before it is saved.

✓

Cycle recording with reasons

Record each cycle as given, modified, delayed, held or discontinued. Every change needs a reason, and the evidence behind it is kept with the decision.

⇪

AI lab report intake

Upload CBP, LFT and RFT reports as PDF or photo. Claude extracts the values, which are cross-checked and confirmed by the clinician. Missing pre-cycle labs are marked.

⌁

Lab trends and flags

Every result is compared with baseline and the previous cycle. Flags cover crossed limits, rapid changes, rising CTCAE grades, KDIGO creatinine rises and projected crossings.

▦

CTCAE v6.0 grading

Grade adverse events with CTCAE v6.0 terms. DailyMed drug labels add context on the known toxicities of each drug in the regimen.

≋

Longitudinal view

Labs, toxicities, patient messages and decisions appear on a single timeline across cycles, with the reasoning shown next to each grade.

✎

Between-cycle review

Symptoms reported by the patient appear in the right assessment window by date. Review a report and record it as a CTCAE toxicity in one step.

⚑

Red-flag triage

Fever, bleeding, breathlessness and other red flags move a message to the top of the inbox, and the patient is told right away to call the team.

How it works

From plan to decision, every cycle

  1. 1Plan

    Create the regimen and review each cycle.

  2. 2Capture

    Record toxicities between cycles, from the clinic or the patient.

  3. 3Labs

    Upload or enter pre-cycle labs. Trends are computed from baseline.

  4. 4Review

    See current and previous labs, flags and CTCAE grades together.

  5. 5Decide

    Proceed, modify, delay, hold, change regimen or discontinue.

The oncologist makes the decision. OncoCare organises the information and highlights what changed. It never recommends a treatment, and AI suggestions always need clinician confirmation.

For patients

Stay in touch with your care team between cycles

  • See your treatment plan and upcoming cycle dates
  • View lab results after your team has checked them, with the original reports
  • Tell your team how you feel by speaking or typing, in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali
  • Read replies from your team in the same place

Not for emergencies

If you have a temperature of 38 °C or more, bleeding, trouble breathing, chest pain, confusion, or you can't keep fluids down, call your care team now or dial 112.
