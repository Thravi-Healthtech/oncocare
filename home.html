The problem

Cancer care is spread across departments, centres and formats.

A single patient's story lives in CT scans on a CD, pathology PDFs, handwritten op notes, lab photos and WhatsApp forwards. Oncologists rebuild it at every visit. OncoCare follows the patient through the whole journey and keeps it in one place.

  1. 01Referral & screening

    Prior reports and scans imported on day one, ABHA-linked.

  2. 02Diagnosis

    Imaging, biopsy, IHC and biomarkers read into structured data.

  3. 03Tumour board

    Case brief assembled automatically; consensus plan recorded.

  4. 04Treatment

    Surgery, chemo, radiation, targeted, immuno and hormonal therapy.

  5. 05Response

    Scans compared over time, toxicities graded, decisions logged.

  6. 06Follow-up

    Surveillance schedule, survivorship and palliative symptom care.

Assistive AI

AI does the reading. The clinician makes the call.

Read

Lab, pathology and radiology reports from PDFs, photos and scanned pages become structured, verifiable data.

Listen

Patients describe symptoms by voice in English, Hindi, Telugu and other Indian languages. Answers are mapped to a fixed question bank.

Track

Every value is compared with baseline and the last visit. Rising grades, rapid changes and missing tests are flagged.

Summarise

Pre-visit and tumour board briefs pull the relevant facts together, each linked back to its source.

Guardrails
  • Never recommends a treatment
  • AI suggestions are highlighted, never pre-selected
  • Every extracted value shows its source document
  • AI never replies to patients directly

Red-flag triage

Fever, bleeding or breathlessness moves a message to the top, and the patient is told to call the team.

The oncologist makes the decision. OncoCare organises the information and highlights what changed. It never recommends a treatment, and AI suggestions always need clinician confirmation.
